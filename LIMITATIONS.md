# Limitations

- This is an educational learning artifact, not a validated research study.
- Sighting reports are user-submitted descriptions and are not evidence that an event occurred as described.
- The original notebook removed missing values and filtered outliers without a documented sensitivity analysis.
- Its spaCy model and dependency versions were not captured.
- The experimental geolocation-prediction section was not reliable enough to present as a portfolio result.
- Any text-derived "emotion" discussion is exploratory keyword analysis, not a clinical or psychological assessment.
